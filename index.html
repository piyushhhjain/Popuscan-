THE IDEA

From rooftops to people — carefully.

Buildings are a useful starting point, but a building count alone is not a population count.

01
▧

Build an inventory

Use satellite imagery or trusted building-footprint datasets to identify structures in a selected boundary.

02
⌂

Understand building use

Separate likely residential buildings from shops, factories, schools, offices and other structures.

03
↗

Estimate occupancy

Combine home counts with local household-size and occupancy information, documenting every assumption.

04
◎

Validate the result

Compare estimates with trusted ground data and show uncertainty rather than presenting false precision.

WHY POUPSCAN?

Make population data more useful for local planning.

Better small-area estimates could help planners explore where schools, clinics, water supply, transport and other services may be needed. These are planning signals, not a replacement for official statistics.

Schools & learningExplore where demand may be higher.
Health servicesUnderstand possible service coverage gaps.
